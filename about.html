Our story

Homely short-stay and holiday homes.

Aces Haven is a family-run business offering short-stay and holiday homes. The brand grew from our love of home, and our wish to create homelike experiences for other people.

Our self-catering and short-term holiday rentals give you a residential base for exploring London and enjoying the local attractions.

We offer secure online booking, helpful hosts, quality homes and homely touches, so you can relax and enjoy your stay.

Our mission

To create memorable, positive experiences by offering homely short-stay and holiday rentals.

Our promise

We never compromise on your satisfaction. At Aces Haven, you can truly feel at home.
